- **`#1` 10-Task Macro `NDCG@10` (`56.40%`, `73.88%` `Recall@10`) on ACL 2025 `CoIR`**: Evaluating all `10 / 10` official code retrieval tasks (`20 / 20` unshrunk sub-corpora = `2,389,123` docs & `162,216` test queries) with `krusch-git` AST Hybrid RRF + `568M` Apache-2.0 `snowflake-arctic-embed2` (`8,192` native ctx), edging proprietary `Voyage-Code-002` (`56.26%` @ `512`-tok Table 3 cap) and `7B` `E5-Mistral` (`55.18%`), alongside strong open-weights retrieval on ICLR 2026 `BEAM` (`100K`–`10M`), `RepoBench-R` (`47,998` queries), and `LegalBench-RAG` (`6,889` span queries);

## 2. How `krusch-stack` Stacks Up Against Three Architecture Archetypes

### A. vs. Standard Open-Source "Wrapper" Stacks *(LangChain / LlamaIndex + Flat 500-Tok Chunks + Stuffed Tool Catalogs + Unguarded Shells)*

1. **Zero-Shot Structural + Centroid Routing vs. Supervised Routers & LLM Preflight**:
   - Supervised routers (`RouteLLM` matrix factorization, `MLP`, `SVM`, `KNN`, `RouterDC`) fit benchmark training distributions well (`61%–66%` ID accuracy on `LLMRouterBench`; `0.7285` `KNN` ID `AIQ` on Martian `RouterBench`), then collapse on unseen out-of-distribution suites (`8.75%` `RouteLLM` and `9.25%` `MLP` on `ARC-AGI`; `KNN = 0.6648`, `SVM = 0.6363`, `MLP = 0.5572` all falling below non-routing `ZeroRouter = 0.6941` on `RouterBench` Leave-One-Suite-Out OOD). `krusch-cascade-router` achieves **`#1` overall `all_test` Pareto distance (`0.4269`)** and **`25.25%` on unseen `ARC-AGI`** (`~2.8x` `RouteLLM`) on `LLMRouterBench`, while **surpassing Martian's `ZeroRouter` OOD ceiling across both `0-shot` (`0.7268` Zero-Shot / `0.7083` Cascade) and `5-shot` (`0.7455` / `0.7389`)** on `RouterBench`.
   - In multi-step coding loops (`Aider Polyglot` `n=60`), invoking a `7B` LLM preflight classifier before every turn burns `569.51ms` per step and misroutes `98.3%` of fresh tasks onto weak models (`21.67%` `Pass@2`). Deterministic failure-taxonomy escalation (`KruschFailureClassifier` + `CascadeRouter`) runs in **`0.395ms` (`1,442x` faster)** and reaches **`51.67%` `Pass@2`** (`+30.00 pp`).
2. **Structure-Preserving Substrates & State Grounding vs. Flat Token Slicing & Raw DDL**:
   - Holding the exact same `snowflake-arctic-embed2` (`1024d`) model constant on `LegalBench-RAG` (`6,889` queries), replacing fixed `160`-token sliding windows (`11.70%` Macro F1) with `KruschNexus`'s hierarchical `Section → Clause` parser, document/section-path contextual headers, and local `mxbai-rerank-large-v2` cross-encoder reranking more than doubles span-exact F1 to **`26.74%` (`2.29x`)**.
   - Across **`2,389,123` code documents** on ACL 2025 `CoIR` (`162,216` queries) and `RepoBench-R` (`47,998` cross-file code queries), `krusch-git`'s AST symbol spine + identifier subtoken FTS5 + dense RRF beats Porter BM25 by **`+7.42 pp` Macro `NDCG@10` / `+9.03 pp` `Recall@10`** (`56.40%` vs. `48.98%`) and pure dense vectors (`55.65%`) by **`+0.75 pp` `NDCG@10` / `+2.17 pp` `Recall@10`** (`+8.00 pp` on multi-turn `codefeedback-mt`, `+4.22 pp` on `synthetic-text2sql`), as well as **`8/8` `RepoBench-R` splits** (`19.12%` vs. `15.69%` Macro `Acc@1`; `68.58%` `Acc@5`).
   - On `BIRD-SQL Mini-Dev` (`500` queries across `11` enterprise DBs), grounding prompts with live `node:sqlite` `PRAGMA foreign_key_list` join edges + `3` sample cell values per column + read-only `Verify-on-Stop` execution feedback lifts execution accuracy from **`26.00%` to `40.20%` (`+54.6%` relative; `5.00x` on `toxicology`, `4.33x` on `california_schools`)** while hitting **`100.0%` valid executable SQL (`500/500`)** at just `0.09` extra calls/query.
3. **Deferred MCP Discovery, Capability Provenance & 2PC Staging vs. Stuffed Unprotected Agents**:
   - Stuffing `64` enterprise MCP tool schemas (`7,894` tokens/turn) into `BFCL v3` degrades abstention on irrelevant requests (`67.23%`). `createDeferredToolBridge` (`krusch_deferred_mcp_guard`) cuts exposed schema tokens by **`-80.67%` (`1,526` tokens)** while **simultaneously increasing overall accuracy by `+6.40 pp` (`66.28%` Micro / `48.72%` 6-Split Macro)**, lifting irrelevance abstention to **`84.69%` (`+17.46 pp`)**, and self-healing `29` schema/type mismatches.
   - Against ETH SpyLab `AgentDojo v1.2.2` indirect prompt injections (`949` `important_instructions` attacks), cryptographic untrusted tool-output spotlighting (`delimitUntrustedContent`) + deterministic sink provenance (`evaluateSovereignToolGuard`) drives targeted ASR from **`19.28%` (`183/949` hijacks; `57.14%` `slack`, `52.78%` `banking`) down to `0.00%` (`0 / 949`)** while **increasing benign utility (`61.86%` → `69.07%`) and utility under attack (`64.49%` → `69.02%`)** on Spotlighting (`61.86%` benign + `83` hardline floor blocks on Sovereign Defense).
   - Staging code edits behind `TruncationGuard` (`0` corrupted files across `225` `Aider Polyglot` tasks) and running 1 `Verify-on-Stop` compiler/test feedback turn heals **`72` initially failing tasks (`+32.00 pp`)** without human intervention.

### B. vs. Commercial Cloud Agent, Router, Code-IR & Memory Stacks *(`NotDiamond`, `Martian`, `Voyage-Code-002`, `past.dev`, OpenAI + Cohere)*

1. **Routing Cost & Latency Frontier**:
   - On official `RouteWorks/RouterArena` CI (`8,400` queries), `krusch-cascade-router` (`0.7883` @ `$0.2117/1K`) outperforms commercial cloud routers including `Azure-Model-Router` (`0.7042`), `Martian` (`0.5756` @ `$4.83/1K` — **`22.8x` more expensive**), and `NotDiamond` (`0.5729` @ `$4.10/1K` — **`19.4x` more expensive**), while on Martian's own `RouterBench` (`arXiv:2403.12031`), `KruschCascadeRouter` retains `98%` of `GPT-4` quality at **`-72.63%` cost** (`$9.89` vs. `$36.15`, nearly `2x` cheaper and `2.8x` faster than `KNNRouter` `$18.71`).
   - During live multi-model generation (`RouterArena` `sub_10`), speculative SSE logprob/loop monitoring (`Path B`) aborts low-confidence `7B` streams mid-flight and replays on `120B`, cutting `120B` mean TTFT by **`-48.0%`** (`1,694.5ms` → `880.7ms`) and total compute cost by **`-37.4%`** even after billing every single aborted token (`58,743` wasted tokens across `325` aborts).
2. **Sovereign Code & Legal Retrieval vs. Proprietary Embedding APIs**:
   - On ACL 2025 `CoIR` (`10` tasks, `2.39M` docs, `162,216` queries), `568M`-parameter Apache-2.0 `snowflake-arctic-embed2` (`8,192` native ctx) + `krusch-git` AST Hybrid RRF reaches **`56.40%` 10-task Macro `NDCG@10` (`73.88%` `Recall@10`)** at `$0` API cost—edging proprietary `Voyage-Code-002` (`56.26%`), `OpenAI-ada-002` (`45.59%`), and `12x`-larger `7B` `E5-Mistral` (`55.18%`) reported in CoIR Table 3 (`512`-token cap).
   - On `LegalBench-RAG`'s `MAUD` merger-agreement suite (`1,676` queries), `KruschNexus` achieves **`33.95%` character-exact `Recall@2`** (`k=2` spans) with `100%` local weights—surpassing the published `LegalBench-RAG` paper's best cloud pipeline (`OpenAI text-embedding-3-large` + `Cohere Rerank` `MAUD` `Recall@64` = `28.28%`) while retrieving `32x` fewer spans.
3. **10-Million-Token Conversational Memory (`ICLR 2026 BEAM`)**:
   - Packing a fixed `~8K`-token (`~69`-turn) slice out of conversations up to `11.6M` tokens (`0.66%` of context) on zero-Docker `node:sqlite`, `krusch-context-mcp v2.0.0` exhibits **zero E2E QA degradation from `100K` → `500K` → `1M`** (`70.60%` → `71.82%` → `72.86%` on `hermes3`; `64.26%` → `62.31%` → `64.64%` on `qwen2.5:32b`) and **outperforms cloud `past.dev` (`openai/gpt-5.6-luna`)** on **Contradiction Resolution across all `4/4` splits**, **`10M` Information Extraction** (`70.00%` vs. `68.75%`), and **`10M` Multi-Session Reasoning** (`29.54%` Qwen / `38.33%` Hermes vs. `28.17%`).

### C. Clean-Room Auditability & Anti-Cheating Controls

Most self-reported AI benchmarks fail basic systems scrutiny due to silent cloud fallbacks, answer-rubric leakage, corpus shrinkage, skipped unit tests, or single-model self-judging. Every benchmark in our suite enforces five deterministic invariants:

- **Loopback Air-Gap (`0` Cloud APIs)**: Open-weights runs execute against `127.0.0.1:11434` with `OPENAI_API_KEY`, `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, and `GEMINI_API_KEY` unset/empty and asserted in the audit log.
- **Runtime `Proxy`, AST Canary & Pre-Oracle Isolation Traps**: Evaluation harnesses wrap benchmark ground-truth fields (`rubric`, `answer`, `SQL`, `ground_truth`, `injection_task_id`, `_id`, `partition`, `meta_information`, `snippets`, `records[model].performance`) in JS `Proxy` traps (`FATAL_ORACLE_LEAKAGE_VIOLATION`) and enforce static source checks prohibiting pre-oracle grading or benchmark-specific literal rules.

## 3. Honest Gaps, Methodology Caveats & Open Upstream Submissions

Publishing only wins hides where open-weights systems still hit real ceilings. Below are the exact gaps and caveats in our current numbers:

1. **ACL 2025 `CoIR` Context-Window Difference (`8,192` Native vs. `512`-Token Paper Table 3 Cap) & Short-Query Task Gaps**:
   - The ACL 2025 `CoIR` benchmark paper (Table 3) evaluated baseline encoders under a `512`-token input truncation cap (`Voyage-Code-002` `56.26%`, `E5-Mistral 7B` `55.18%`), whereas `snowflake-arctic-embed2:latest` (`568M` Apache-2.0) runs at its native `8,192`-token context window (`56.40%` Hybrid RRF / `55.65%` Pure Dense). Longer context materially lifts long multi-turn code QA (`codefeedback-mt` `60.23%`) and contest code translation (`codetrans-contest` `95.98%` / `97.96%`), whereas short web/competitive query tasks (`apps` `9.70%` vs. `26.52%` `Voyage-Code-002`, and `cosqa` `23.76%` vs. `32.76%`) still favor proprietary code-pretrained models.
2. **Aggregate `BEAM` E2E Reader Ceiling (`70.6%–73.5%` Open-Weights vs. `86.2%` `Hindsight` / `92.1%` `past.dev`)**:
   - **Cross-Family Referees vs. Cloud Self-Judging**: Our `BEAM` E2E scores grade open-weights `gpt-oss:120b` (`5.1B` active parameters) using two independent cross-family open-weights referees (`qwen2.5:32b` and `hermes3:latest`, `81%–88%` binary agreement). By contrast, `past.dev`'s `92.1%` leaderboard entry uses `openai/gpt-5.6-luna` as **both** the reader and its own judge.
   - **Strict Ordering Rubrics (`Event Ordering`: `9.68%` Qwen / `43.17%` Hermes vs. `100%` `past.dev`)**: `krusch-context-mcp v2.0.0` retrieves **`81.19%`** of gold `Event Ordering` turns at `100K`, yet `qwen2.5:32b` awards `0.0` whenever an ordered list of 6–10 milestones shifts by a single position—creating a `33.49 pp` inter-referee grading spread on identical model outputs.
   - **Passive `supersedes_id = NULL` Benchmark Ingestion (`Knowledge Update`: `37.5%–52.5%` at `100K` vs. `86%–92.5%` `past.dev`, despite `97.50%` Recall)**: To avoid tuning benchmark-specific write heuristics, our `BEAM` runner ingests raw chat turns passively with `supersedes_id = NULL`, leaving superseded historical turns active inside the `8K` context window where they distract the reader. In live IDE sessions, agents explicitly call `krusch_context_revise({ action: "supersede" })` to archive obsolete decisions (`status = 'superseded'`), eliminating stale-turn competition at the storage layer.
3. **Frontier Multi-Turn Reasoning Ceiling on Hard Coding (`51.56%` `Polyglot`) & Complex SQL (`40.20%` `BIRD-SQL` vs. `65%–75%+`)**:
   - Single-retry `Verify-on-Stop` (`Pass@2`) lifts open-weights `gpt-oss:120b` from `19.56%` to **`51.56%` (`116/225`)** on `Aider Polyglot` (tying `Claude 3.5 Sonnet` `51.6%` and beating `GPT-4o` `23.1%`) and from `26.00%` to **`40.20%` (`201/500`)** on `BIRD-SQL Mini-Dev` (`39.35%` on the `460` `challenging` queries, `50.00%` on `moderate`), all at `$0` API cost. However, multi-turn frontier reasoning pipelines (`o3`, `gpt-5`, `Claude 4`, `Gemini 4 Argon`) reach `75%–88%+` on `Polyglot` and `65%–75%+` on `BIRD-SQL`—which is why `krusch-cascade-router` escalates repeated local verification failures to L3 frontier models on the hardest ~15% of tasks.
4. **Monolingual Zero-Variance Feature Spike on `RouterBench` `0-Shot OOD` Leave-`chinese`-Out (`0.0811` vs. `#1` `0.4880` ID & `0.3059` `5-Shot OOD`)**:
